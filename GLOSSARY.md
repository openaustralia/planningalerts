# Glossary

**Commenting disabled**:
A staff decision that stops new comments, either from one person or on one application. Existing comments stay as they are, and drafts already started can't be published, so nothing more is sent to the authority. For a person, they keep receiving alert emails. For an application, it applies to everyone, admins included. People are told commenting is "turned off", for their account or for the application. When both apply, the person's message is shown.
_Avoid_: banned, suspended, blocked, comments closed, comments locked, comments frozen

**Hidden comment**:
A published comment removed from public view by staff. Separate from commenting being disabled for the person who wrote it.
